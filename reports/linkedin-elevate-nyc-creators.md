## Hook options (recommended first)

1. We didn't invite creators to Elevate to film the keynote. We invited them to build.
2. The best moment of Elevate NYC for me happened in a workshop room, not on the main stage.
3. Twenty creators, one day at Elevate NYC, and one rule: don't watch AI agents from the audience, build one.
4. Most creator programs at conferences give people a pass and a good seat. We tried giving them a laptop and an hour with our product team.

## Body

[Hook]

Bringing creators to @monday.com Elevate NYC this year was a different kind of comms project. No press release, no briefing pack. Just a day designed so they could experience the product and the people behind it, then tell the story in their own voice.

The day started with coffee and a guided headphone tour of the event, then the opening keynote, and then the part I was most excited about: a hands-on workshop where every creator built their own AI agent with our product team. We closed it with a community dinner together with our ambassadors and team.

## Changelog (v1 → v2)

Pepper isn't working and this session has no Bright Data access, so this round was benchmarked with a regular web search instead of a live scan of LinkedIn posts. The sources are mostly marketing blogs. They are directional, not measured data.

- **Hook order.** Moved "We didn't invite creators..." to first because the guides advise opening with the insight, not the event name. Added hook 4 as a contrast option. ([LinkedInPreview](https://linkedinpreview.com/blog/linkedin-conference-post-2026))
- **Creator tags moved to the first comment.** 21 tags in the body looks like spam-tagging and can hurt reach. The body now tags only the team and the two agencies. ([Writio](https://writio.ai/blog/how-to-get-linkedin-posts-seen-by-people-outside-your-network-2026), [SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/blog/linkedin-algorithm/))
- **Added a placeholder for one real takeaway.** A recap that adds your own point of view gets more engagement than a list of thank-yous. The takeaway is left for you to write so nothing gets invented. ([Writio](https://writio.ai/blog/how-to-write-linkedin-post-after-attending-conference))
- **Cut "Can't wait to see what they create."** It's generic. The closing question now does that work.
- **Length unchanged.** About 170 words without the comment, inside the 150 to 300 range the guides recommend. ([The Shield Index](https://theshieldindex.beehiiv.com/p/does-linkedin-post-length-actually-matter))
