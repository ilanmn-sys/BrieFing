# Elevate London creator workshop: UK creator pool (research, 30.10.2026)

Read-only research for Ilan. Nobody was contacted. All numbers below came from sources retrieved on 30.10.2026. A number that wasn't retrieved is marked n/a.

## 1. Method

**Discovery.** About 100 Google SERP calls through Bright Data (`marko_serp_json`, gl=uk). Some searched by keyword ("UK/London AI tools creator", "British tech YouTubers AI productivity", "UK AI educator instagram", "London-based AI content creator", X/Twitter AI accounts). Others looked for roundup lists (Feedspot, Modash, Disrupt Marketing, Favikon UK Top 200, creatordb). The rest checked named candidates one by one. SERP worked poorly for finding micro creators on IG and TikTok. Most usable names came from roundups, speaker bios, and searches on individual names.

**Verification.** Seven Bright Data Web Scraper runs, all batched:

- Instagram Profiles: 22 inputs.
- TikTok Profiles: 12 inputs.
- YouTube Channels: 16 inputs. All 16 failed with `HPE_HEADER_OVERFLOW`, so I used YouTube Videos discovery instead.
- X Profiles: 18 inputs. This run returns a cached older post set.
- Instagram Posts: 15 profiles × 12 posts = 180 records.
- YouTube Videos: 13 channels × 10 videos = 100 records. Three channels failed.
- X Posts (discovery, 01.08–30.10.2026): 10 profiles, 34 records.

A TikTok Posts discovery run (7 profiles × 12) was still building when I wrote this. TikTok ER below therefore comes from the TikTok Profiles dataset's `top_posts_data`, which holds the 12 most recent posts by `create_time`. That field gives likes only.

**Verified vs SERP.** "verified" means the follower count came from a Bright Data dataset record. "SERP" means it came from a Google snippet and is unverified. LinkedIn was not scraped (blocked by policy), so every LinkedIn number is SERP-only.

**Engagement-rate formulas.** Posts sampled are the latest available. On IG I left out pinned posts older than 180 days where enough recent posts were returned.

- IG: ER = (likes + comments) / profile followers, averaged per post. I also give the median, because single viral posts skew the mean.
- TikTok: ER = likes / followers. Likes only, because comments and shares weren't returned.
- YouTube: ER = (likes + comments) / views per video. I also give average views.
- X: ER = (likes + replies + reposts) / followers on posts from 01.08–30.10.2026. Only 1–5 posts per account were returned, so treat X ER as indicative.

Every ER cell shows the platform and N.

**UK basis.** I accepted a bio location, an X location field, a uk.linkedin.com profile URL, or press or speaker bios. If none was found, the cell says so.
