# Elevate London creator workshop: UK creator pool (research, 30–31.10.2026)

Read-only research for Ilan. Nobody was contacted. All numbers below came from sources retrieved on 30–31.10.2026. A number that wasn't retrieved is marked n/a.

## 1. Method

**Discovery.** About 130 Google SERP calls through Bright Data (`marko_serp_json`, gl=uk). Some searched by keyword ("UK/London AI tools creator", "British tech YouTubers AI productivity", "UK AI educator instagram", "London-based AI content creator", X/Twitter AI accounts). Others looked for roundup lists (Feedspot, Modash, Disrupt Marketing, Favikon UK Top 200, creatordb). The rest checked named candidates one by one, and looked up cross-platform handles and LinkedIn counts. SERP worked poorly for finding micro creators on IG and TikTok.

**Verification.** Bright Data Web Scraper runs, all batched:

- Instagram Profiles: 22 + 5 inputs.
- TikTok Profiles: 12 + 3 inputs.
- X Profiles: 18 + 3 inputs.
- YouTube Channels: 16 inputs. All 16 failed with `HPE_HEADER_OVERFLOW`, so I used YouTube Videos discovery instead.
- Instagram Posts: 15 profiles × 12 posts = 180 records.
- YouTube Videos: 13 channels × 10 videos = 100 records. Three channels failed.
- X Posts (discovery, 01.08–30.10.2026): 10 profiles, 34 records.
- TikTok Posts (discovery): 7 profiles, 82 records.

**Cross-platform links.** I fetched the public link-in-bio pages and personal sites (linktree, bio.site, willfrancis.com, aiwithkyle.com, charliehills.ai, bernardmarr.com, azeemazhar.com and others) with plain HTTP GET requests, to confirm that accounts on different platforms belong to the same person. These were read-only requests to the creators' own public pages. No LinkedIn page was fetched.

**Verified vs SERP.** "verified" means the follower count came from a Bright Data dataset record. "SERP" means it came from a Google snippet and is unverified. "self-reported" means the creator's own bio or site. LinkedIn was not scraped (blocked by policy), so every LinkedIn number is SERP or self-reported.

**Engagement-rate formulas.** Posts sampled are the latest available. Every ER cell shows the platform and N.

- IG: ER = (likes + comments) / profile followers, averaged per post. I also give the median, because single viral posts skew the mean. Pinned posts older than 180 days were left out where enough recent posts were returned.
- TikTok: ER = (likes + comments + shares) / followers over the 12 most recent posts from the TikTok Posts dataset, mean and median. The TikTok average views column is average plays.
- YouTube: ER = (likes + comments) / views per video. I also give average views.
- X: ER = (likes + replies + reposts) / followers on posts from 01.08–30.10.2026. Only 1–5 posts per account were returned, so treat X ER as indicative.

**Total followers.** The sum of the counts in the cross-platform table (section 5), across IG, TikTok, YouTube, X and LinkedIn. It is a **reach sum, not unique audience**. Newsletter and podcast audiences are listed but not added. Each total shows how much of it is dataset-verified.

**UK basis.** I accepted a bio location, an X location field, a uk.linkedin.com profile URL, or press or speaker bios. If none was found, the cell says so.
