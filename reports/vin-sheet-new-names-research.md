  - LinkedIn posts since 25.09 for the same 9: sd_mwapjwj97kd0qpoek. Jordan returned no posts. The 12-month LinkedIn back-scan (sd_mwapohbg2o52ouszhw) returned nothing usable, because the scraper only sees the latest ~15 posts on a profile.

  - TikTok for @huwprosser and @futurebriefing: still running when I wrote this, see Data gaps.
- **Web search** for identity, location, employers and podcast sponsors.
- **ER** = (likes + comments) / followers, per post, using today's follower counts. I give mean and median over the latest ~12 posts, and **score on the median**. LinkedIn ER runs structurally lower than IG or TikTok (0.1–0.5% is normal there even for strong creators), so the ≥2% bar is harsh on LinkedIn-first people. **V** = verified today by a scrape or the person's own profile. **S** = search snippet, self-reported, or Ilan's input.
- **Scoring** follows the brief: Fit / UK proof / Engagement / Clean (R-39), 0–2 each. Totals are out of 8 because fees are not in yet. Clean = 0 means drop.
  - Competitors: Asana (hard no), ClickUp, Notion, Wrike, Smartsheet, Atlassian (Jira/Confluence/Trello/Loom), Airtable, ServiceNow, Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Attio, GoHighLevel and similar.
  - AI-agent or "AI employee" products (Viktor, Grok Bot, Manus) are not competitors.
- **NYC benchmark:** working tech professionals explaining AI for work, IG Reels first, 20K–500K, face-to-camera. High = all three; Med = two; Low = one or none.
