Every number below says where it came from: **Sprout** (Sprout Social API, owned accounts), **BD dataset** (Bright Data Web Scraper API, per-post metrics), **SERP** (Google via Bright Data, discovery only, no metrics), **Board** (monday.com news board 446791074). `n/a` means the source did not return the number; nothing is estimated.

## 1. Top line

- **223 Elevate mentions counted** after a strict relevance check: 13 media articles and 210 social items (103 earned, 107 owned, of which 58 are Instagram stories).
- **Split by platform:** Instagram 103, LinkedIn 55, X 20, YouTube 13, Facebook 8, Threads 5, TikTok 4, Reddit 2, media 13. Podcasts: 0 found.

- **Top 3 themes (judged):** (1) AI agents moving from assisting to doing the work: the keynote, "Over 1,000 agents run from scratch" on day 1 (Elevate by monday.com), Elevate Labs build sessions and the creators' agent workshop; (2) the enterprise-deployment story: monday Frontier (managed services and forward-deployed engineers), permissions and trust, and the customer stories on theCUBE (HPE, Lowe's, MPA); (3) the in-person experience: the expo, partner booths, the Ryan Reynolds fireside chat and Lindsey Vonn's appearance, and the scale ("6,000 in the room, 200,000+ signed up online").
- **Standout pieces:**
  - Elevate '26 Opening Keynote on the Elevate YouTube channel: 302,515 views (BD dataset). Biggest single item. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1gS7o8In73w
  - SiliconANGLE / theCUBE package: 4 Tier 2 articles (Board), 8 interview videos with about 78k views combined (BD dataset), and 2 LinkedIn posts. theCUBE is a **paid media partner** of Elevate; SiliconANGLE discloses this in the articles.

  - Cruz Roja Venezolana: a customer story covered by 2 Venezuelan outlets plus 3 posts from the organisation (IG 384 likes). This is the only LATAM press found.
- **Negative or risky items:**
  - Mild complaint on Facebook (Mary O'Shaughnessy, 29.10): "great #Elevate26 but this Microsoft session really needed to be at the Main Stage" (6 comments). Room capacity, not product.
  - UC Today's preview (28.10) had a sceptical framing: it will be "worth checking" whether agents deliver. Its 31.10 analysis also noted gaps ("Monday left some..."). Welcome.AI's AI-generated summary of the preview ran as "monday.com Faces Challenges with AI Agents Amid Competitive Landscape" and lists AI agent costs as a concern.

  - All theCUBE/SiliconANGLE coverage is sponsored (disclosed). Treat it as paid earned media.

## 6. Methodology and limits

- Clock: `date` read 2026-11-01 17:54 UTC before starting; window 25.10 to 01.11.2026 inclusive.
- Board: monday.com news board 446791074 read with the monday MCP (read-only GraphQL). Publish date (`date`) between 2026-10-25 and 2026-11-01 returned 29 items. None were marked Website? = "Not coverage". 5 are about Elevate and 24 are not.
- Slack: #monday_in_the_news (C010G6MMCLB) read for the window. It held only member-leave events and no coverage posts. Public-channel search for Elevate found internal posts only (watch parties, booth logistics, one employee LinkedIn link). Slack's internal figures (e.g. "almost 6K attendees", "110K on the live stream") are not used as mention data.
- Sprout Social (customer 1671587): metadata/customer for owned profiles; analytics/posts for the 9 owned profiles (monday.com X, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, monday CRM LinkedIn, Elevate by monday.com LinkedIn, monday.com Japan X), created 25.10 to 01.11, lifetime metrics. That returned 162 posts: 39 X replies and 14 Facebook ads were excluded, and 34 posts plus 58 IG stories passed the Elevate filter. analytics/profiles was used for daily follower change. Read-only calls only.
- Google via Bright Data SERP (zone marko_serp_json, gl=us, hl=en, date range 25.10 to 01.11): 138 queries (web plus Google News) covering site: queries for linkedin.com/posts, linkedin.com/pulse, instagram.com, tiktok.com, youtube.com, x.com and twitter.com, facebook.com, threads.com, reddit.com, Apple Podcasts and Spotify; the phrases and hashtags monday.com Elevate, monday Elevate, #mondayelevate, #elevate2026, #elevate26, Elevate '26, elevate.monday.com and Javits; exec and speaker names; Hebrew and Spanish variants; and one query per roster creator. 519 unique result links were returned. Google's redirect links were resolved to the real URLs.
- Bright Data Web Scraper API (datasets/v3 trigger, progress, snapshot) for per-post metrics: Instagram Posts (36 URLs, all returned), X Posts (6), TikTok Posts (2 URLs, plus keyword discovery for #mondayelevate, #elevate26 monday and monday.com elevate, plus a profile scan of the 9 roster creators with TikTok accounts for 25.10 to 01.11), YouTube Videos (17), Facebook Posts by URL (5), Threads Posts (5), Reddit Posts (2). LinkedIn was never scraped and no LinkedIn dataset was run.
- Creator roster and found.json (agents/elevate-nyc-content-watch/state) were used as the creator list. Today's dataset values replace the logged ones where they were re-scraped.
- Article pages were opened (plain HTTP fetch) to check the Elevate mention and the publish date for board items and search finds; 5 returned 403 (Kan, Office Snapshots, Milenio via Muck Rack, Times of Israel blog, Seeking Alpha).
- Sentiment: the board's status2 where it is set, otherwise my judgement from the text. All social sentiment is judged.

**Relevance filter (strict).** An item counts only if it mentions Elevate (the conference, Elevate '26, #mondayelevate, or #elevate2026/#elevate26 together with monday, or elevate.monday.com) or is clearly about something announced or happening at Elevate NYC. Each counted item has a `why` note in the JSON. Dropped by this filter:
  - News board: 24 of 29 window items are not about Elevate. These include the Harmony voice AI launch (4 pieces: none mentions Elevate, and Globes dates the launch to the week before), stock columns, roundups and the layoffs tracker. They are listed in the JSON as other monday.com coverage (26 with 2 search finds).
  - Owned (Sprout): 17 posts dropped. These are 9 monday.com YouTube uploads (ad creatives "Build With Me", "From campaign chaos..." and a test upload), emoji-only reposts, a Docusign product post, the AI report post, the "Customer zero" repost, and "Can't say we're not committed" on LinkedIn and IG (posted during the event, 8,673 and 10,734 impressions, but no Elevate in the text; likely event content, check manually).
  - Social candidates screened out after reading the caption: itskormenia (no Elevate in caption) and a YouTube video titled "Elevate 26" with no monday tie.
  - Search false matches discarded before scoring: other events called Elevate (FintechOS Elevate '26 London, Mindtickle Elevate '26, Helvar ELEVATE'26, Elevate Summit NY, UFOF Elevate 26, ElevateUAV, Elevate Academy, Elevate Digital Optics, Elevate Nation, Honda Elevate), a Ugandan "Monday Elevate" dance class on TikTok with #MondayElevate, "Monday" the weekday (NFL Monday Night, Monday outfits and menus), the verb "elevate" (sports and fashion), TikTok discover pages (aggregations, not posts), community.monday.com threads, monday.com's own website pages and an undated partner newsletter (XTIVIA).

**What couldn't be measured**
- LinkedIn: discovery through Google only (no scraping), so coverage is partial and biased towards posts Google indexed within days. Reactions and comments are n/a for earned posts except counts visible in Google snippets (marked). Elevate by monday.com page analytics returned nothing from Sprout.
- X: Google indexes few X posts, and the X dataset needs post URLs (no keyword search), so earned X volume is a floor. Only 6 earned posts were found.
- Instagram: the Posts dataset did not return reel view counts for earned posts, so those are n/a (Creator Buzz reported about 26k views for Maitri's reel; that figure is not used). Like counts were hidden on 2 posts (n/a). Hashtag-level discovery isn't available: the "Instagram posts search by keyword" dataset refused collection.
- Stories: live 24h stories of creators and third parties can't be read. Owned stories come from Sprout. Mariana Antaya's 27.10 story was shared in Slack but can't be measured, so it is not counted.
- Private accounts, closed Facebook groups and comments inside other people's posts are not visible.
- TikTok: SERP returned few TikTok posts. Keyword discovery returned 91 posts, mostly 'Monday'/'elevate' false matches; 2 natalieintx videos (28.10, @mondaydotcom #elevate) passed. The roster profile scan returned 25 posts from the window across 6 of the 9 creator accounts (3 had none). None mentions monday.com or Elevate, so there is no creator TikTok to count. Captions only: on-screen text and audio aren't checked.
- Podcasts: no podcast episode mentioning monday.com Elevate was found (Apple Podcasts and Spotify site queries returned nothing relevant).
- Media: 5 pages returned 403 (3 board items, 2 search finds), so the Elevate check for those relies on title and snippet. Seeking Alpha's publish date couldn't be verified. Media reach and UVM aren't on the board columns used and weren't estimated.
- Sprout Listening: GET /listening/topics returned 404 twice, so no Listening volume or sentiment is available (the account seems not to have Listening via API). All earned social comes from Bright Data and search.
- Facebook: 3 items (2 videos and Maitri's cross-post) are SERP-only, so their metrics are n/a. Owned Facebook in the window was ads only.
- Engagement totals add up only what each source returned. They are not comparable across platforms (Sprout engagements include clicks; BD likes don't).

Raw data: reports/elevate-nyc-mentions-2026-10-25_11-01.json (every counted item with source, metrics, `why`, plus dropped candidates and Sprout daily profile data).
