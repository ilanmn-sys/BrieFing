## Method and sources

- Bright Data Web Scraper API, one batched run per platform: Instagram Profiles `gd_l1vikfch901nx3by4`, Instagram Posts `gd_lk5ns7kz21pck8jpis` (for engagement), TikTok Profiles `gd_l1villgoiiidt09ci`, X Profiles `gd_lwxmeb2u1cniijd7t4`, YouTube Channels `gd_lk538t2k2p1k3oos71`. Follow-up runs only for newly found accounts, the new group, and YouTube retries.
- Google SERP via zone `marko_serp_json` to find missing profile URLs and all LinkedIn URLs (LinkedIn was never scraped). A LinkedIn URL was accepted only when the result title/snippet matched the name and the niche/brand (or the URL came from the creator's own bio).
- Reused prior research: `agents/elevate-nyc-content-watch/state/roster.json` and `reports/london-creators-research.md`; links on the same creator's other CRM item (exact same person, other group).
- Followers = the primary platform's count (the first platform in the Primary Platform label). Avg ER = mean over the latest ~12 posts of (likes + comments) / followers × 100, on the primary platform only. Computed for Instagram (Posts dataset) and TikTok (profile videos). Not computed for YouTube (dataset has no per-video likes), X (fewer than 6 posts since 05.2026 in the dataset), LinkedIn (not scraped).
- Website: only a link the creator lists in a profile link field (IG external links, TikTok bio link, X link, YouTube links) whose domain matches their name/handle. Domains that only appeared inside bio text (often email domains) were not used. Link-in-bio pages (Linktree, Beacons, Stan, bio.site) were not put in Website.
- Language: Hebrew if the bio/captions are in Hebrew, English if clearly English (TikTok predicted_lang used as support). Region was already filled on every item.
