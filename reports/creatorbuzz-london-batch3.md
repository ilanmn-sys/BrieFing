    - Grace `sd_mwja3nur1rs5idjm3s`: 11 records, 7 of them hers
    - Plus the 5 specific sponsored posts by URL: `sd_mwja6f6y26uq8hkvy5`
  - YouTube Channels `gd_lk538t2k2p1k3oos71`: `sd_mwja4h52vfljv55xy`
  - YouTube Videos `gd_lk56epmy2i5g7lzu0k`, 15 videos: `sd_mwja4fyi12i3rf9xeu`
- **SERP:** Bright Data `marko_serp_json`, gl=uk, 33 queries. They covered handles, TikTok/YouTube presence, competitor terms, sponsors and controversy.
  - Post dates were decoded from the LinkedIn activity IDs and the IG shortcodes.
- **ER:** (likes + comments) / followers per post, median.
  - Grace's IG uses her 12 posts from 06.10 to 15.11 that show likes; 5 hide likes. Her 3 pinned older posts (two viral collabs at 62K and 102K likes, plus one from 08.04) are excluded.
- **Reach estimates.** These are not measured. Neither platform returned views, so I estimated reach from the engagement we do have:
  - **IG reel:** median likes ÷ a 2–5% like-to-view ratio gives **~30–70K views**.
  - **IG stories:** 3–5% of followers gives **~5–8K per frame**.
  - **LinkedIn post or video:** reactions ÷ a 1–2.5% reaction-to-impression ratio gives:
    - Grace: **~15–35K** impressions. Her one video in the sample (20.10) got 220 reactions, below her median.
    - Mischa: **~15–38K** impressions.
  - **CPM** = price ÷ (estimated reach / 1,000).
- **Duplicate check:**
  - Influencers CRM 2026: no item for either creator (name filter: Grace, Andrews, Mischa, Collins).
  - Elevate List:
    - **Mischa Collins already existed** in LONDON CREATORS (13471026768, created 05.11 from Vin's UK sheet).
    - "Grace Beverley" (13552596661) is a different person: Grace Andrews is covering Grace Beverley's Working Hard podcast during her maternity leave.
  - reports/:
