- **What is Certification Day?** Tue 27 Oct at the Javits Center, about 4 hours of training, for General Admission + Certification or VIP + Certification passes. Bring a laptop and charger. The exam is taken online afterwards, not on site.
- **Accessibility, diet:** see Venue & logistics. For specific requests, use the contact form.
