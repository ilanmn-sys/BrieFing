## New York: Javits Center
- Address: 429 11th Avenue, New York, NY 10001
- Getting there: subway, bus, taxi and rideshare. Level-access entrances on all sides. Drop-off details are coming closer to the event: TODO.
- Badge pickup: Wed 08:00–10:00 and Thu 08:00–09:30. Location and ID needed: TODO.
- Security: every bag is searched. Wheeled luggage, luggage with telescoping handles, or bags bigger than about 18" × 14" must be stored. Prohibited: weapons, drugs, drones, pets (service animals are allowed), tripods, monopods and selfie sticks. Confiscated items are not returned. The whole campus is smoke- and vape-free.
- Professional photo and video gear: only for media-credential holders or with prior approval.
- Stages: Main Stage (keynotes) · Vantage Stage (600 seats) · Horizon Stage (250 seats, fills fast) · Showcase Stage (150 seats, live demos). Masterclasses are in the Learning Labs, roundtables in the Collab Labs.
- Expo floor: AI Hive, Expert Bar, monday Hub, Product Lab, Braindate Lounge, Spark Lounge (registered only). There is a VIP Lounge for VIP passes.
- Event app: schedule, venue map, networking, live updates, and the Elevate Explorer game in the Klik tab. Download instructions come by email.
- Food: catered stations labelled for allergens and diets (gluten-free, vegan, vegetarian, nut-free, halal, kosher). Severe allergies are flagged at registration.
- Accessibility: accessible restrooms on every floor; reserved seating near accessible entrances in general sessions; mobility devices and service animals are welcome; ASL at the keynote; closed captions on all stages; a private nursing room on site. For specific needs, use the contact form.
- Laptop: recommended for hands-on sessions.
